3. On-Page SEO Conducted

  • Primary keyword placed in the homepage title and H1.
  • Primary keyword placed in the main article title and introduction.
  • Secondary keywords placed naturally in H2 headings and article paragraphs.
  • Long-tail keywords included in relevant sections.
  • SEO-friendly meta titles and meta descriptions added.
  • Meta keyword lists added for assignment demonstration.
  • Internal links added between Home, Blog, Article, About and SEO Report.
  • Canonical tags included.
  • Open Graph metadata included on key pages.
  • Responsive/mobile-friendly design maintained.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml included.
