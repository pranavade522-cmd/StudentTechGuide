About StudentTechGuide

StudentTechGuide is a college SEO project created to demonstrate practical keyword research, on-page SEO and content optimization around AI tools for college students.

Project Objective

The project demonstrates how a student blog can be structured and optimized using a primary keyword, secondary keywords, long-tail keywords, descriptive titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links and measurable search performance.

Target Topic

The main topic is AI tools for college students, with supporting topics such as AI study tools, AI tools for studying, AI tools for engineering students and free AI study tools.
